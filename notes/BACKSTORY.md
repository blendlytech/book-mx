# Backstory

I grew up in a world where alcohol was a staple.  It was everywhere.  At every celebration, at every meal, at every gathering.  It was a normal part of life.  You're 12?  Have a beer.  You're 16?  Have a few.  You're 18?  You're an adult.  Drink 'til you drop.  That's what we were taught.  That's what we were shown.  That's what we were told.  There was no moderation.  There was no control.  There was no balance.  There was just drinking.  And then there was being drunk.  And then there was waking up the next day and doing it all over again.

I have 3 older sisters, none of which caught the bug, so to blame it on my upbringing would be ridiculous.
