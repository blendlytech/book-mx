# Marriage and Child

 We got married after only knowing each other for 2 months much to our families dismays.  She was joining the Army.  We were in love, we thought.  Truth is we didn't know each other and were in lust.  This was our first duty station. Stripped apart from everyone we knew and told "good luck".  We lasted 3 years.  I over drank, got into spice, a synthetic form of marijuana in order to pass drug screens at my Home Delivery Manager job with FedEx Home Delivery.  I was a complete alcoholic and I couldn't put the spice down.  It really grabbed a hold of me.  It ruined our marriage and my ability to fight to have my daughter in my life.  I choose the bottle and the drug over a healthy, loving relationship with a baby I loved more than anything in this world.  You wouldn't know it by my decision to continue using, but I didn't see it that way.  In the end, my ex-wife baby momma had every right to be cautious.  I was given every opportunity to win back my child, but I failed, because I thought I knew better than everyone else and that everyone else was just trying to hurt me out of resent and hate.

 I was not a functioning alcoholic.  I was a functioning drunk.  There is a difference. A functioning drunk is someone who can hold their liquor and go to work every day.  A functioning alcoholic is someone who can't stop drinking and it's ruining their life.  I was the former, or so I told myself.  I always thought of myself as more outgoing, funnier, sociable when drinking.  I felt I lost all that sober.  Anyone who had a problem with my excessive drinking just weren't drinkers and didn't understand it.  They had their own problems I always thought.  Don't worry about me.
