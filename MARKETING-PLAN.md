# A Broken Compass — 30-Day Launch Plan

## Non-Negotiables

1. **Author location is never public.** No current city/state in bio, Amazon Author Central, or social profiles. Geotags off on every post. No identifiable windows, street signs, plates, or landmarks in any real footage or audio. The pen name stays separate from personal accounts.
2. **Faceless visuals only.** Hands, boots, silhouettes, POV, objects, landscapes. No AI-generated faces of Clay, Glenda, Omar, or anyone real. AI imagery is *mood*, never *reenactment*.
3. **Clay's real voice narrates everything** via ElevenLabs voice clone. The voice is the proof that the story is true.
4. **AI content label ON** for every TikTok / YouTube / Meta post.
5. **Imply violence, never show it.** No drugs on screen, no weapons, no blood. Psychological dread only. (Keeps reach from being throttled.)
6. **Only real lines from the book.** No invented quotes in marketing copy.

## Production Pipeline

1. **Style bible:** 10 reference stills in Gemini Image (compass, map, bar top, Baja hills, mountain bus, kitten, headset desk). Every clip is animated *from a still* (image-to-video) for consistency.
2. **Base style string** for every prompt: `Vertical 9:16. Handheld, 35mm film grain, desaturated amber and dust tones. No people's faces. Slow, deliberate camera motion.`
3. **Voice:** ElevenLabs Professional Voice Clone of Clay. SFX from ElevenLabs (wind, chains, slot machines, phone lines).
4. **Assembly:** CapCut, burned-in captions, hook text on screen within the first 1.5s, end card "Follow for stop X."
5. **Batch on weekends.** Never generate on posting day.

## Zero-Budget Engine

Paid reach is replaced by four free channels:

1. **Organic short video** (series above). Volume is the budget: 2 posts/day from Nov 16.
2. **Engagement on other people's posts:** 15 min/day of real comments on #SoberTok, #RecoveryTok, #MemoirTok, and true-crime creators' videos, plus stitches/duets. That's how a new account gets discovered without ads.
3. **Podcast guesting.** Recovery, true-crime, and survival podcasts need guests every week. A cartel-kidnapping survivor with a sober story is an easy booking. Audio-only interviews keep the author's face and location out of it.
4. **Communities.** Facebook recovery groups and Reddit, *only where the rules allow self-promotion*, and lead with the story, not the link. One banned account burns the whole community.

**Free tooling:**
- Captions: TikTok/Instagram/YouTube built-in auto-captions (CapCut auto-captions may be behind a paywall).
- Newsletter: free tier of MailerLite or Kit (check current subscriber limits).
- ARCs: EPUB attachment by email (readers use Send to Kindle). Skip BookFunnel/BookSirens.
- Link-in-bio: free tier of Linktree, or the Amazon page directly.

**Amazon review rules (ARCs):** reviewers must disclose the free copy, can't be asked for a positive review, and reviews from close friends/family may be removed. Recruit strangers from the recovery/true-crime audiences.

## KPIs & Kill Rule

- 3-second hold under 60% → rewrite the hook.
- Track shares + saves over likes; profile clicks; preorders/day.
- A series that is flat after 5 posts gets cut; double down on the winner.
