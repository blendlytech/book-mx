# Book: MX (Survival & Recovery Memoir)

[![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Drafting-orange.svg)](#)
[![Genre](https://img.shields.io/badge/Genre-Memoir--NonFiction-red.svg)](#)

A gritty, high-stakes, first-person survival and addiction memoir documenting a chaotic real-life timeline stretching from the telemarketing floors of Las Vegas, through substance abuse, family estrangement, and active warrants, to a dangerous flight across the Mexican border, captivity in the Rosarito mountains, and the long road to recovery.

---

## 📖 Project Overview

This repository serves as the central manuscript workspace for drafting, formatting, and refining the memoir. The project captures the unvarnished reality of active addiction, the psychological toll of survival, and the slow process of redemption without sanitizing the protagonist's voice or decisions.

### Core Narrative Arc
```
Las Vegas (Scams & Relapse) ➔ Flight from Warrants ➔ Calexico/Mexicali Border Crossing ➔ Rosarito Captivity ➔ Mexicali Rehab ➔ Recovery
```

## 🎨 Editorial Guidelines (The Voice)

The writing style of the memoir is governed by strict editorial constraints:
1.  **Voice Preservation**: The tone is raw, conversational, and direct. Avoid corporate sanitization, moralizing, or clinical framing of substance abuse and scams.
2.  **Show, Don't Tell**: Expository summaries must be expanded into sensory scenes (e.g., the specific smell of the squad car's *Joop* cologne, the feeling of walking in cheap orange jail moccasins, the heat of a Vegas afternoon).
3.  **Tension Modulation**: Fast, fragmented sentences capture action and immediate threats (like the dog chase in the Prologue); slower, introspective prose details moments of recovery, family loss, and addiction reflections.
